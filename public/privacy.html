Privacy Policy

FamOs (working title) · Effective date: 14 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kseniia Vorobeva, an individual developer based in the United Arab Emirates (“we”, “us”, the “Operator”), collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the FamOs application and website (the “App”). By using the App you agree to this Policy.

Contact for any privacy question or data request: support.famos@gmail.com

1. What the App is

FamOs is a family-organization app that helps a household coordinate in one place: shopping lists and a home inventory, recipes and a nutrition/meal diary, a family and partner calendar, tasks and chores, habits, an optional menstrual-cycle tracker, a medicine list, and roles for co-parents, children, and household staff who are invited by the account owner.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: email address and display name used to create and sign in to your account.
  • Profile details you choose to add: date of birth, sex, height/weight and goals, avatar photo.
  • Household content you create: calendar events, tasks and chores, shopping lists and pantry inventory, recipes, meal/nutrition diary entries, habits, medicines, notes, and profiles you add for children or staff.
  • Health-related data (optional features): if you use them — menstrual-cycle dates, body weight, and food/calorie logs. You choose whether to enable these.
  • Photos: images you add for recipes, inventory scanning, avatars, or notebook/word scanning.
  • Technical data: a user identifier and basic app/diagnostic data needed to operate and secure the service.

We do not use advertising trackers, and we do not collect an advertising identifier (IDFA). We do not ask for tracking permission because we do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

3. How we use your information

  • To provide the App’s features and sync your household data across the members you invite.
  • To power optional AI features you trigger (for example, estimating a meal from a photo or text, recognizing groceries from a fridge photo, or scanning words from a photo).
  • To secure your account, prevent abuse, and fix problems.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your health-related data for advertising.

4. Service providers (sub-processors)

We use trusted providers to run the App. They process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and file storage (your account and household data).
  • Vercel — hosting of the web app.
  • OpenAI — when you use an AI feature, the relevant photo or text is sent to OpenAI to generate the result. It is used to fulfil your request and not for advertising.
  • Nutrition data sources (e.g., USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts) — used to look up food/nutrition information when you search.

5. Children’s data

FamOs is intended for adults who organize a household. A child’s profile and, where applicable, a child login are created and controlled by a parent/guardian — children do not sign themselves up. Sensitive functions are parent-gated. The App is not directed to children for independent sign-up, and we do not knowingly collect data directly from a child without a parent setting it up. A parent can edit or delete a child’s profile at any time, or delete the whole account (see “Your rights”).

6. Your rights and choices

  • Access & correction: you can view and edit your data in the App.
  • Deletion: you can permanently delete your account and personal data from within the App (Settings → account → “Delete account”). This removes your login and personal data; shared household data is handled as described in the App at the time of deletion.
  • Requests: for any access, correction, or deletion request, email support.famos@gmail.com.

Depending on where you live (e.g., the EU/UK under GDPR), you may have additional rights, including to object to or restrict processing and to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

7. Data retention

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account, your login and personal data are deleted. Backups, if any, are cycled out in the ordinary course.

8. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and access is restricted by per-user database security rules. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

9. International transfers

Our providers may process and store data in countries other than yours. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Effective date” above and, where appropriate, notifying you in the App.
